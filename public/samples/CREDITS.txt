Salamander Grand Piano, recorded by Alexander Holm.
Original instrument: Yamaha C5 grand piano.
Source MP3 distribution: https://tonejs.github.io/audio/salamander/
Original creator: https://rytmenpinne.wordpress.com/sounds-and-such/salamander-grandpiano/
The creator states: "As of 4.3.2022, this is now public domain!"
Historically distributed under Creative Commons Attribution 3.0:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/

This app bundles nine unmodified MP3 samples from that distribution.
Playback transposes the closest sample to the requested pitch and applies
velocity gain and a release envelope. No endorsement is implied.
